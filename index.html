Optimization and decision-making under uncertainty for large-scale infrastructure systems.

I am a Research Fellow in the Structural/System Reliability Group at Seoul National University. I develop optimization methods to support decisions in energy systems and regional-scale infrastructure under uncertainty.

My goal is to bring a systems-level perspective to infrastructure that shapes people’s lives, connecting modeling and assessment with better decisions.

01 / Research

Decision-making under uncertainty

Two connected research areas address different sources of uncertainty, using assessment and optimization to support better decisions.

01

Disaster Risk & Resilience of Regional Infrastructure

My research focuses on disaster risk and resilience assessment of regional infrastructure, considering uncertainty in hazards, damage, and recovery. I use optimization to support investment and recovery decisions; my building retrofit work uses buffered failure probability to prioritize investments under severe loss scenarios.

Research details

I formulated a mixed-integer regional retrofit model and led its numerical analysis and first-author manuscript preparation, linking probabilistic risk assessment to resilience-oriented decisions.

02

Renewable Energy Systems under Climate Change

I assess the reliability of renewable-dominant energy systems under climate change, considering uncertainty in climate projections, weather variability, renewable supply, and demand. I connect these inputs with network-constrained dispatch to assess power shortfall risk and support planning and operational decisions.

Research details

Climate-to-grid assessment integrates network-constrained DC-OPF for a reduced Great Britain transmission network, measuring daily shortfall probability and severity. Exploratory grid-planning research considers inherited storage conditions and investment decisions.
