Privacy & visit statistics

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This academic website is operated by Uichan Seok. Contact: uichanseok@gmail.com.

Your choice

Optional Google Analytics 4 (GA4) helps me understand visits and page views. The Google analytics tag is blocked until you choose “Allow analytics”. Declining does not limit access to any website content. No analytics request, including a cookieless consent ping, is sent before consent or while your choice is declined.

Use “Analytics settings” in the main page footer to change your choice. Declining after allowing stops further analytics on this browser and clears this site’s analytics cookies; it does not erase data already received by Google. Your choice is stored locally for up to 180 days. If browser storage is unavailable, it applies only to the current page.

Information Google can receive

After you allow analytics, Google can receive page views, visit times, a cookie-based browser identifier, session and engagement information, and basic browser, device and language information. Requests also expose your IP address to Google; Google can derive approximate geographic information from it. Detailed location and device collection is disabled in the property settings. We do not request precise location. Google may process information on servers outside your country.

This site sends the page origin and path with query strings and URL fragments removed. The referring page is omitted. It does not intentionally send your name, email address, form entries or a signed-in user ID to Analytics. Advertising personalization and Google Signals are disabled. There are no advertising links or custom form, outbound-click, download, search or scroll tracking events in this implementation.

Cookies and storage

After consent, GA4 may set _ga and _ga_… cookies to distinguish browser visits and sessions. This site requests a 180-day cookie lifetime without refreshing it on each visit. Browser policies may shorten that lifetime. The local storage entry academic-analytics-consent-v1 stores only your allow/decline choice and its date.

Data retention

GA4 user and event data retention is set to 2 months, with retention resets for new activity disabled. Aggregate standard reports can be retained beyond this period. These settings are separate from the 180-day browser cookie and consent-choice lifetime.

Access to reports

Analytics reports are intended for the site owner’s Google account. They are not published on this website. Google processes the analytics data to provide its service. Read How Google uses information from sites that use its services, Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s consent mode documentation for more information.

Website hosting and external links

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Hosting requests are separate from optional analytics and may be processed by GitHub for operation and security under the GitHub Privacy Statement. External links are governed by their destination sites’ policies.
